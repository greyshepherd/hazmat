# Brand assets

Generated by `Scripts/make-brand-assets.swift`, which is the source of truth for
the geometry. Run it from the repository root to regenerate all three files.

The mark is the mask: Hazmat replaces Gas Mask, and the successor wears the mask.
A bone face piece with two glass lens ports and a chin filter that breaks the
silhouette, set on a deep petrol tile. The tile is the platform's own icon shape,
so the icon carries no corner geometry of its own.

- `Hazmat.icns` — the app icon, drawn at each size from 16 to 1024 pixels rather
  than downscaled from a master.
- `menu-bar-template.png` and `@2x` — the mask alone at 22 and 44 points, its
  lens ports punched through. Pure black plus alpha: the system tints it for
  appearance and highlight, and the app never recolours it.

`Scripts/assemble-bundle.sh` copies all three into the bundle's
`Contents/Resources` and names `Hazmat.icns` in the bundle's property list.
